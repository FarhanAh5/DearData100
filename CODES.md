# My codes

I'm recording everything I eat and drink, to see how much of it I make at home and how much
I buy out.

Every meal, snack, coffee, bottle of water - all of it. One line per thing.

## The line

```
date,time,place,kind,cost
```

Like this:

```
19/10,13:30,B,S,7
```

That's a snack I bought on the 19th at 1:30pm, cost me £7.

## What the letters mean

**place** - where it came from

- `C` cooked it at home
- `B` bought it out
- `G` someone gave it to me

**kind** - what it was

- `M` meal
- `S` snack
- `D` cold drink
- `H` hot drink

**cost** - roughly what I paid. `0` if it was free or from home.

## Rules I'm sticking to

- Write it down straight away, not at the end of the day.
- Count everything, even a glass of water.
- Don't skip the embarrassing ones.
- Don't change these codes halfway through.

## A change I made partway through

I started on 19th Oct recording only meals and snacks. After a few days I realised I was
missing a big part of what I actually have in a day, so from 22 October I started counting
drinks as well: water, tea, coffee, all of it.

So the rows before 22 October have no `D` or `H` lines in them. I left those days as they
are rather than guessing.
