# My codes

I'm recording everything I eat and drink, to see how much of it I make at home and how much
I buy out.

Every meal, snack, coffee, bottle of water - all of it. One line per thing.

## The line

```
date,time,place,kind,cost,after
```

Like this:

```
19/10,13:20,B,M,8.50,1
```

That's a meal I bought on the 19th at 1:20pm, cost me £8.50, and I was glad I had it.

## What the letters mean

**place** - where it came from

- `C` cooked it at home
- `B` bought it out
- `G` someone gave it to me

**kind** - what it was

- `M` meal
- `S` snack
- `D` cold drink
- `H` hot drink
- `T` treat

**cost** - roughly what I paid. `0` if it was free or from home.

**after** - how I felt after

- `1` glad I had it
- `0` fine, nothing either way
- `-1` regret

## Rules I'm sticking to

- Write it down straight away, not at the end of the day.
- Count everything, even a glass of water.
- Don't skip the embarrassing ones.
- Don't change these codes halfway through.
